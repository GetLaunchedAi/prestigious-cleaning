About Us

About Prestigious Cleaning and Sanitation LLC

Prestigious Cleaning and Sanitation LLC is a trusted cleaning and sanitation company proudly serving businesses and facilities across Michigan. We specialize in comprehensive janitorial services, industrial sanitation, and post-construction cleaning designed to meet regulatory standards and exceed client expectations.

With a strong background in the sanitation and manufacturing industry, we quickly realized that reliable cleaning businesses were hard to come by—most struggled to consistently deliver on communication, promises of service, and integrity. Too often, one of these pillars had to be sacrificed for the other. At Prestigious Cleaning and Sanitation LLC, we take pride in holding these values to the highest standard and making them a core foundation of our business.

Our mission is simple: create cleaner, safer, and healthier spaces for our clients. By leveraging advanced cleaning systems, high-quality equipment, and a commitment to excellence, we deliver consistent results you can depend on. Above all, communication, delivering on our promise of service, and working with integrity are what set us apart.

I’m Lam Nguyen, owner of Prestigious Cleaning and Sanitation LLC. Our team takes pride in integrity, responsibility, and transparency in every project we handle. We treat each facility as if it were our own—ensuring a spotless, sanitized environment every time.
